Celtics rotation board

Who was on the floor, minute by minute, and a blank board to build next season's rotation. One cell is one minute; green means on the floor.

Right-click or long-press the image to save it.Download

On the 2026-27 plan tab, the "On floor" strip counts players per minute: red means fewer than five, green means exactly five, and the board won't let you add a sixth. Shift-click fills a run of minutes in one row. Avg is minutes per game played, the same way Basketball Reference computes it; untick a game to leave it out. Tap a cell to toggle a minute on or off. Rename a row to project someone new into that slot (Brown → George, a big → Robinson). Add a row for anyone who doesn't map onto last year's shape and draw their stints in.
